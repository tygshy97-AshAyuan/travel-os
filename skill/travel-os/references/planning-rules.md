# Research and planning rules

## Understand the trip before recommending

Capture purpose, traveler relationships, dates, entry and exit cities, confirmed transport, budget range, pace, comfort, mobility, food and attraction preferences. Ask only questions that can change the route. Treat MBTI or astrology as optional entertainment context, never as evidence about a person.

## Research with distinct evidence roles

- Official sources decide opening hours, transport rules, entry requirements and safety restrictions.
- Recent traveler content can reveal photo positions, crowding, hidden details and practical experience; label it as experience, not authority.
- AI reasoning connects the evidence into a route and explains trade-offs. It is not a source.

For time-sensitive facts, record URL or source name, checked time and fact state: Confirmed, Unverified, Stale or Conflicting.

## Build the route from large to small

Choose country or region, entry city, exit city, route shape, city nights and intercity transport before daily attractions. Minimize unnecessary backtracking while respecting confirmed bookings and user priorities.

## Evaluate attractions for this user

For each shortlisted place capture why it matters, historical context, core experience, photographic value, uniqueness, cultural value, time cost, suggested duration, best position or detail, constraints and fallback. Reject places whose value does not justify their route or time cost.

## Transport and accommodation

Choose entry and exit transport before hotel locations. Compare low-cost flights by baggage, seats, comfort, transfer time, airport distance and total cost, not fare alone. Choose accommodation in relation to the next day's route, airport or station access, parking, view and room experience; explain the travel-time consequence.

## Produce an executable plan

Include realistic transfers, buffers, meals, rest, check-in rules, luggage, accessibility, reservations and alternatives. Do not fill every hour. A plan is ready only when hard constraints and confirmed bookings are reconciled and remaining uncertainty is visible.
