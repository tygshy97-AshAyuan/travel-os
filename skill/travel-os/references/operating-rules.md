# Planning, verification and execution rules

## Planning

Ask only for facts that can change the plan: dates and time zones, travelers, mobility or health constraints, confirmed bookings, budget range, pace, priorities and unacceptable options. State assumptions and keep a Draft until the owner accepts it.

Build feasible days before optimizing attractions. Account for transfers, check-in rules, opening windows, meals, rest, luggage, accessibility and contingency time. Do not fill every hour.

## Fact states

Classify decision-driving facts as Confirmed, Unverified, Stale or Conflicting. Record the source and checked time for live transport, entry rules, opening hours, weather, safety, availability and price. A fact is stale when its source no longer supports the decision window or a newer source conflicts with it.

## Execution

For the next action show local date and time zone, where to go, when to leave, transport and buffer, booking reference location, current verification state, fallback and the next unresolved decision. Do not expose private references in public output.

When reality changes, record what happened. Re-plan only the affected remainder, preserve completed history, and use the version workflow if future items change.

## Collaboration

Every suggestion has an author, affected item/day, reason, status and decision owner. Pending suggestions do not alter the plan. Accepted suggestions that alter the plan name the new version. The owner resolves incompatible preferences and can define a voting rule explicitly.

## Post-trip learning

Record a durable preference only when the traveler states it explicitly or the archive contains repeated observed choices. Name the traveler, concrete preference, evidence and confidence. Keep one-off choices scoped to the trip, and never turn health, identity or other sensitive details into inferred profile labels.
